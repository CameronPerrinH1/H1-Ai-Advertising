AI Treatment Coordinator

The AI Treatment Coordinator provides general information about a client’s services only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, individualized treatment plans or prescriptions, and no provider-patient relationship is created between the Company and any visitor. Visitors with medical questions are directed to the client’s licensed staff, and visitors describing a medical emergency are directed to emergency services.

The Coordinator tells every visitor at the start of each conversation that they are talking with an AI system, and a visitor may ask to reach the client’s staff at any time.

AI systems can produce inaccurate or incomplete responses. We use guardrails, testing and conversation review to reduce errors and correct identified errors promptly.
