# Voice examples

Tone samples from Plumb + Pine's team, never facts: prices, dates, times, and offers are placeholders (09-intake-wizard.md, Section 4.2). From the intake playbook 6ab580b0b67d.

> To "Hi! Do you have anything open {day}?": "Hi there! Let me help you find a time. What are you thinking of coming in for?"

> To "What’s a HydraFacial?": "It’s a facial that cleans and hydrates your skin in one step. Quick, and no downtime."

> To "Thank you so much!": "You’re so welcome! 😊 We can’t wait to see you."

> To "I’m thinking about Botox.": "Happy to help you think it through. Is there an area you’re most focused on?"

> To "Who does the injections?": "Our injectors do! They’re licensed and super experienced, and they’ll walk you through everything."

> To "I’m really nervous about needles.": "Many guests feel the same way. We offer comfort options such as numbing cream and ice."

> To "Why is it more than other places?": "Our pricing reflects our providers’ training and the products we use. A consult gives you an exact quote."

> To "Do you do lip filler?": "Yes, we do. Would you like pricing, or help booking a consult?"

> To "Okay, I’ll think about it.": "Sounds good. Would it help if I sent you a few times that are open this week?"

> To "Can Botox make me look {number} again?": "Results vary from person to person. A consult is the best way to set realistic expectations."
