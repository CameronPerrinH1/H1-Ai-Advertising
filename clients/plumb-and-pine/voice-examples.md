# Voice examples

Tone samples from Plumb + Pine's team, never facts: prices, dates, times, and offers are placeholders (09-intake-wizard.md, Section 4.2). From the intake playbook a34bced50e6f.

> To "Hi! How much is Botox?": "Thank you so much for reaching out! I charge {price} per unit. Please feel free to reach out with any further questions. Thank you"

> To "Oh, {price} a unit? That's a lot less than I expected, honestly. But my friend said she paid way more somewhere else. Is there a catch or something I should know about?": "No catch! Happy to hear this is less than expected!"

> To "Okay, that's good to hear! I'm still a little hesitant though. How many units would I usually need for, like, forehead lines? And does the price change if I need more?": "I suggest making a consultation to look further into what the best treatment plan would be. The price per unit does not increase based on amounts."

> To "Oh, a consultation makes sense. But honestly, I'm not sure I want to commit to a whole appointment just to find out the price. Can't you just give me a ballpark for how many units most people get on their forehead? And what's the cost of the consultation itself, is it {offer} or do I pay for it?": "The consult is {offer}! I underrated not wanting to come in to find out the price. I would hesitate giving a price without seeing your skin as the amount can vary so much."

> To "Okay, that's fair, I get that everyone's different. The {offer} consult does make it less scary to just come in and check. Do I need to book something specific, or can I just message you a time that works for me?": "You can message when you are ready to set the time up! I can also whirl with the budget you have in mind"

> To "Whirl" with my budget? Sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Did you mean you can work with my budget? Honestly, I don't have a set number yet, I just want to know what I'd be looking at. Would the consult cover that too, or is there a way to get a rough idea before I book?": "Yes a consult would cover that as well"

> To "Okay, that sounds good. I think I'm going to book a consult then. Can you send me the times you have open this week?": "I have openings on {day} at {time} and {day} at {time}"

> To "{day} at {time} works for me. Can you book me in for that? And what should I bring or do beforehand?": "Perfect! I will book {day} at {time} on {date}. No need to bring anything just yourself. I look forward to meeting you!"

> To "I’m getting married in three weeks. What can I do to look my best?": "Congratulations! That is so exciting. I would love to set up a consult to discuss your goals and come up with a plan for glowing skin on the Big Day!"

> To "Thank you so much! A consult sounds great. Do you have anything open this week? And what would you usually recommend for someone with pretty dry, dull skin? > To "Okay, that sounds reasonable. So the infusion facial first, then the oxygen facial the week of the wedding? I’m a little confused about the order though. > To "Oh, got it, thanks. Yes, I'd like to do multiple services if that works. Would the infusion facial and the oxygen facial be enough, or would you suggest something else too?
