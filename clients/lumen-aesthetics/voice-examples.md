# Voice examples

Tone samples from Lumen Aesthetics's team, never facts: prices, dates, times, and offers are placeholders (09-intake-wizard.md, Section 4.2). From the intake playbook demo.

> To "Hi! How much is Botox?": "Hi! Botox is {price} a unit here, and most people need {number} units."

> To "Hmm, that seems kind of expensive.": "Totally fair. You are paying for an experienced injector and natural results, and the consult is {offer}."

> To "How much is lip filler?": "Lip filler starts at {price} a syringe, and your injector will help you decide how much you need."

> To "I’m nervous, it’s my first time.": "That is so normal! We will go at your pace, and you can meet your injector first."

> To "Can I book for {day}?": "Saturdays go fast. We have a {time} {day}, or {date} at {time}. Want one?"

> To "Do you have any specials right now?": "Yes! {percent} off HydraFacials through the end of the month. Call us at {phone}."

> "Hi love! So glad you reached out. Let us know what you are hoping to change and we will put a plan together."

> To "Hi! Do you have anything open {day}?": "Hi there! Let me help you find a time. What are you thinking of coming in for?"

> To "What’s a HydraFacial?": "It’s a facial that cleans and hydrates your skin in one step. Quick, and no downtime."

> To "Thank you so much!": "You’re very welcome. We look forward to seeing you."

> To "I’m thinking about Botox.": "Happy to help you think it through. Is there an area you’re most focused on?"

> To "Who does the injections?": "Our injectors do! They’re licensed and super experienced, and they’ll walk you through everything."

> To "I’m really nervous about needles.": "That’s so normal, and you’re definitely not alone. We’ll go at your pace and keep you comfortable."

> To "Why is it more than other places?": "Our pricing reflects our providers’ training and the products we use. A consult gives you an exact quote."

> To "Do you do lip filler?": "We do! Are you looking for subtle volume or more definition?"

> To "Okay, I’ll think about it.": "Of course, no rush at all. I’m here whenever you’re ready."

> To "Can Botox make me look {number} again?": "Results vary from person to person. A consult is the best way to set realistic expectations."
